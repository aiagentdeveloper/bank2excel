# Marketing Playbook (based on Starter Story data)

The "PDF bank statement converter" niche: comparable businesses do $40K/mo with a $100 start and 14-day build. Revenue is NOT guaranteed — this playbook maximizes the odds. Free tools: SEO, Reddit, Product Hunt, X, influencers.

## 1. Product Hunt launch (week 1)

- Create the Gumroad product + license flow first so the paywall works
- Submit to Product Hunt with a demo GIF (upload → summary → download in 20 seconds)
- Prep the "maker comment": the story of why boring tools win (Packager $60K/mo, CutCoach $20K/mo examples)
- Ask 10–20 friends/communities to upvote on launch day
- Expected: first 5–20 license sales if it ranks

## 2. Reddit (highest intent, free)

Post in: `r/Accounting`, `r/Bookkeeping`, `r/smallbusiness`, `r/ecommerce`, `r/finance`, `r/personalfinance`, `r/smallbiz`, `r/solopreneur`

Content (no overt ads — value first):
- "I'm tired of manual data entry from bank PDFs — built a converter" + honest demo
- "How do you convert bank statements to Excel?" threads → answer with the tool in comments
- r/Accounting Friday threads ("tools you use") — get mentioned

Rules: never spam; 1 post per sub per 1–2 weeks; answer questions genuinely.

## 3. SEO (compounds over months)

The comparable business in Starter Story grew on SEO. Target keywords:
- "bank statement to excel" / "convert bank statement to excel"
- "pdf to excel bank statement" / "bank pdf to csv"
- "statement converter" + bank names (chase statement to excel, revolut statement to csv...)
- "how to convert bank statement to excel" (how-to articles)

Add a `/blog/` later with 1 article/week. Schema.org FAQ/HowTo markup. Internal links to the converter.

## 4. X / TikTok (the LeadFlow/Shipper pattern)

- 3–5 posts/week: screen recording of a 20-second conversion + a pain point
- "POV: your accountant sent you a 40-page PDF again" style hooks
- Reply to "bank statement" complaint threads with the tool
- Hashtags: #accounting #smallbusiness #microsaas #buildinpublic

## 5. Influencers (small, cheap)

Starter Story's CutCoach pattern: 1K–10K view creators, DM partnerships, pay per view (~$2–3 CPM with view guarantee). Niche: accounting/bookkeeping YouTubers and TikTokers. One good video → 10K+ revenue (PepAI case).

## 6. Pricing & upsell

- $9 lifetime is a low-friction impulse price (Gumroad license)
- Later: $19 "Pro" tier with OCR for scanned PDFs — the #1 feature request
- Add affiliate program (Gumroad built-in) for accountants who refer clients

## 7. Metrics to watch (first 60 days)

- License sales per week (Gumroad dashboard)
- Conversion rate free → paid (paywall in-app; goal: >5%)
- Which channels drive signups (add `?ref=` to Gumroad links per channel)

## Launch sequence (14 days)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Gumroad product live, paywall tested end-to-end |
| 4–5 | Product Hunt draft + demo GIF |
| 6–8 | 5 Reddit posts + replies |
| 9–11 | X/TikTok posts daily, DM 10 small creators |
| 12–13 | Fix feedback: parser edge cases, feature requests |
| 14 | Product Hunt launch day |

## Honest expectations

Boring-niche tools win on **specificity and persistence**: comparable founders took 2–24 months to reach $10K+ MRR. The product is the 20% — distribution is the 80%. Do the Reddit/PH loop weekly for at least 3 months before judging.

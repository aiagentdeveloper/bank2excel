Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we store

Nothing sensitive. Bank statements are processed in memory and the resulting files are deleted from our servers automatically 15 minutes after conversion — or immediately after download.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not store, copy, or sell your statement contents.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not share data with third parties (except Gumroad for payment processing, which receives only your purchase email and license key).

Cookies

We use one short-lived cookie to enforce the free-tier limit (1 conversion per browser per day). No tracking cookies.

Payments

Payments are processed by Gumroad. We never see your card details. Your purchase email is used only to validate your license key.

Contact

Questions? Contact us through the Gumroad product page.
